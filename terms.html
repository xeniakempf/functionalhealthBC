Do not start, stop or change any treatment based on website content. Using the website, contacting us, or booking a free discovery call does not create a nurse practitioner–patient relationship.

Emergencies

We do not provide emergency or urgent care. If you have a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For non-urgent health advice at any hour, call HealthLink BC at 8-1-1.

Messages sent through the patient portal or by email are not monitored around the clock and should never be used for urgent concerns.

Who can use our services

  • You must be 18 years or older.
  • You must be physically located in British Columbia at the time of every consultation. We may ask you to confirm your location, and we may need to reschedule if you are outside BC.
  • You agree to give accurate, complete information about your identity, health history and medications, and to tell us about changes.

Our care and your relationship with us

A nurse practitioner–patient relationship begins when you complete your initial appointment and we agree to provide care. We may decline to provide care, or end a care relationship, where treatment is not clinically appropriate, outside our scope, or where these terms are not followed. If that happens we will give reasonable notice where possible and help you find other care.

We focus on hormone therapy. We do not replace your family doctor or nurse practitioner, and we encourage you to keep one for general and preventive care.

Virtual care

All consultations take place by secure video or phone. Virtual care has limits: we cannot do a physical examination, and technical problems can interrupt a visit. If we decide that you need an in-person assessment, we will tell you.

Please join from a private place with a reliable connection. You are responsible for the privacy of your own device and surroundings.

Prescriptions and lab testing

  • Prescribing is always at the nurse practitioner’s clinical judgment. Booking an appointment does not guarantee a particular prescription.
  • Hormone therapy requires appropriate testing before treatment and regular monitoring during it. Continuing a prescription depends on attending follow-up visits and completing recommended bloodwork.
  • Some medications, including testosterone, are controlled substances and are prescribed only in line with federal law and BCCNM standards.
  • Medications are filled by the pharmacy you choose. Lab tests are performed by independent laboratories. We are not responsible for their services, pricing or availability.

  • Lab and medication costs, if any, are separate and are billed by the lab or pharmacy.
  • Cancellations: please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Appointments cancelled with less notice, or missed, may be charged in full.
  • We may change our fees with notice posted on the website. Changes do not affect appointments already paid for.
